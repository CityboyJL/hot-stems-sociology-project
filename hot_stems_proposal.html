Section 02

The Format

🎬

Act 1 — Hot Stems Interview (30–60 sec)

Animated teaser screened school-wide. Guest interviewed on 5 escalating questions — personal, professional, societal. Each wing = a harder truth. Inspired by Hot Ones.

🎙

Act 2 — Live Podcast Recording

Speaker joins a student-run podcast in the school media room. Unscripted, real, raw. Students submit questions in advance. Recorded for the school's YouTube channel.

📱

Social Campaign — @hotstems.official

Each event generates an Instagram post with the speaker's key quote + stat. Shared on school channels. QR code posters link to full episodes hallway-wide.

🏫

Student-Led — From Pitch to Publish

Students handle outreach, moderation, filming, social, and the reflection showcase. This is advocacy in action, not just awareness on paper.

Section 04

4-Week Schedule

Week 1 · Feb 2–6 — Launch
Campaign kickoff assembly. Teaser screened school-wide. Hallway posters + QR codes deployed. Instagram page launches. Stat poster: "28% — the number that has to change."
Week 2 · Feb 9–13 — Episode 1: Engineering
Dr. Mojgan Daneshmand. Teaser screened Monday. Live podcast Thursday (gym/media room). Recording published Friday to school channel + Instagram.
Week 3 · Feb 17–20 — Episode 2: Innovation (Family Day week)
Dr. Gitanjali Rao (virtual). Student challenge: submit a 1-sentence STEM invention idea. Best idea read live during the podcast recording.
Week 4 · Feb 23–27 — Episode 3 + Reflection Showcase
Final speaker + library showcase. "What will YOU burn down?" sticky-note wall. Campaign wrap post with student voices featured.
Section 05

Goals & Success Metrics

🧠

Awareness

100% of students exposed to at least one fact about gender bias in STEM through posters, assemblies, or class screenings.

🎤

Connection

At least 3 live speaker conversations, each attended by 50+ students. No panels, no scripts — just real talk.

📣

Reach

Instagram campaign targets 500+ engagements per post. Podcast episodes shared beyond school walls to parents and community.

💬

Action

End-of-series reflection: students complete a 3-sentence written response. Anonymous survey measures attitude shift toward STEM inclusion.

Dear [Principal's Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am a Grade 12 student in the Social Advocacy course. I am writing to propose a student-led school initiative called Hot Stems — a speaker series designed to raise awareness about gender bias in STEM and inspire our students to see science and technology as fields that belong to everyone.

The issue is real and urgent: only 28% of STEM workers in Canada are women (Statistics Canada, 2023), and research shows that 40% of girls who are qualified for STEM careers never consider entering them. This gap does not start at the workplace — it starts in classrooms like ours, when students don't see themselves reflected in the people who lead these fields.

What is Hot Stems? Inspired by the viral interview format Hot Ones, the initiative brings real women researchers and STEM leaders into our school for a two-part experience: a short animated teaser interview that screens school-wide, followed by a live student-moderated podcast recording at the school. Each session also generates a social media post connecting our campaign to a wider audience.

We are proposing four weeks in February 2026 (Feb 2–27), featuring three speaker sessions and a student reflection showcase in Week 4. The entire initiative is student-run — from outreach and moderation to filming, social media, and the final showcase. We are not asking for large funding; most of the event can be held in the media room or gym at no cost.

I have attached a full proposal deck outlining the schedule, proposed speakers, goals, and success metrics. I would welcome the opportunity to present this to you in person at your convenience. I am confident this initiative will leave a lasting impact on how our students think about who belongs in STEM.

Thank you sincerely for your time and consideration.
